Barbecue Tools That Outlast One Summer

Good grilling relies on reliable tools and a clear understanding of how heat works, not on marketing hype or unnecessary gadgets.

The fashionhotspot editors10 picks

Grilling feels simple: fire, food, friends. But many tools promise to simplify it further, often adding clutter instead. Most people buy their first grill, then slowly accumulate accessories. This often means buying redundant items or things that fail quickly. A basic kit of reliable tools will serve you better than a drawer full of single-use gadgets. Focus on durability and core function over flashy features.

The biggest trap in grilling gear is believing that more expensive means better results. Many common tasks, like flipping burgers or checking doneness, do not require specialized, high-tech equipment. You can achieve excellent results with basic, well-made items. Instead, invest in tools that accurately measure conditions or genuinely protect you from heat. Skip anything that claims to make food taste better through some obscure mechanism.

Common questions

Is expensive charcoal worth it?

For most backyard grilling, standard briquettes or lump charcoal are perfectly adequate. Expensive 'gourmet' charcoals often promise hotter burns or unique flavors. While some lump charcoal burns cleaner with less ash, the difference in flavor for short cooks is often negligible, especially if you are also adding wood. Save your money on premium charcoal for very specific, long smoking projects where fuel quality might make a small difference. For burgers and steaks, basic charcoal works just fine. Focus more on temperature control than the brand of fuel.

Should I buy a specialized grill cleaning spray?

Most specialized grill cleaning sprays are not necessary and often contain chemicals you might prefer to avoid near your cooking surfaces. For routine cleaning, a good bristle-free brush and warm, soapy water are usually sufficient. For stubborn, caked-on grease, try heating the grates, scraping them thoroughly, then letting them cool before scrubbing with a heavy-duty degreaser or even a paste of baking soda and water. Always rinse thoroughly if using chemicals. The best 'cleaner' is actually consistent cleaning after each use, preventing buildup in the first place.

Do I need a separate set of grilling tools from my kitchen tools?

A separate set of grilling tools is highly recommended. Grilling tools are often longer, sturdier, and designed to withstand higher temperatures than typical kitchen utensils. They also tend to get dirtier and develop a smoky residue that you might not want to bring into your kitchen. Items like a long-handled spatula, tongs, and a meat fork are indispensable for safely handling food over an open flame. While a basic kitchen spatula might work in a pinch, it risks burning your hands and often lacks the necessary reach or strength for grilling tasks. It's an investment in safety and convenience.

Are grill mats or grill toppers useful for non-stick cooking?

Grill mats and solid grill toppers can prevent food from sticking and keep small items from falling through grates. However, they create a barrier between your food and the direct flame, which can reduce charring and prevent the authentic grilled flavor and sear marks many people desire. They turn your grill into more of a flat-top griddle. If you want maximum grill flavor and browning, it's better to invest in a good grill basket for small items or ensure your grates are very clean and well-oiled. Use mats only if you prioritize non-stick over full char.
