Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

Information We Collect

fashionhotspot does not require registration and does not ask you for personal data. We do not run advertising trackers or sell data. Standard web-server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) are retained briefly for security and performance purposes only.

Local Storage

The site stores one value in your browser's local storage — your chosen interface language — so the site opens in that language next time. It never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

Affiliate Links

This site contains links to Amazon and AliExpress. We have applied to the Amazon Associates Program and are not yet a member, so no commission is currently earned on Amazon links. Where an affiliate relationship does exist, buying through a link may earn us a commission at no additional cost to you. Clicking any such link takes you to the merchant, who then applies their own cookies and privacy policy.

Prices and Availability

Prices shown on this site are captured at the time of listing and may change. The price displayed on the merchant's own page at the moment of purchase is the one that applies. Always verify the current price before buying.

Third-Party Links

We link to third-party sites such as Amazon and AliExpress. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices; please review their policies directly.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your Rights

Because we do not hold personal accounts or profiles, there is generally no personal data to access or delete. If you believe we hold information about you, write to us and we will respond.
