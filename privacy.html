Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

You can browse AllyFind without giving us anything. Nothing on the site asks for your name, email address or any other personal detail, and there is no account, sign-up form or mailing list at the moment. This page says what is (and is not) recorded when you visit.

Email addresses

We do not collect email addresses through this website. If you write to us at support@allyfind.com or contact@allyfind.com, we use your address only to reply, and delete the conversation on request.

If we ever add a mailing list, it will be an opt-in form that says what it is for, and this page will be updated before the form goes live. What we commit to now: we would store only the address, the date and the page language; keep it on our own web hosting, not sell or share it; and put an unsubscribe link in every message.

Everything else we record

Server logs. Our web host keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and to run the service. We do not use them to identify or profile visitors.

Deal clicks. When you click a deal, the link passes through a small redirect script that adds one to a click counter for that deal and that day. It stores no IP address, no browser details and nothing else about you.

Cookies and your browser. We set no cookies of our own and use no analytics or advertising trackers. To remember your choices the site keeps a few settings in your browser’s local storage on your own device — language, currency, view and page-size preferences, and the deals you recently viewed or saved. They are never sent to us and you can clear them in your browser at any time.

Other services your browser contacts

Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads. Product photos load from AliExpress’s image servers. If you click a deal you leave this site for AliExpress, which has its own cookies and privacy policy. Links to WhatsApp open WhatsApp. We do not control any of these services.

Affiliate Links

This site contains affiliate links to AliExpress. Some links on this site are affiliate links. Where an affiliate relationship does exist, buying through a link may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you.

Third-Party Links

We link to third-party sites (AliExpress). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their respective privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, or to have anything you sent us deleted: support@allyfind.com
