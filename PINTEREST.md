# Pinterest plan — fashionhotspot

Generated 2026-08-17 from `content/*.json`, so every URL and title below is real.

## Why Pinterest specifically

It is a **search engine**, not a social feed. Users arrive intending to buy, pins
keep returning traffic for years rather than dying in a day, and every guide
already has a hero image at `images/hero-<slug>.jpg`. It is the only channel on
the list that pays back months after you stop working on it.

**Do this first:** claim the domain at Settings → Claimed accounts → Claim website.
It unlocks analytics per pin and puts your logo on every pin sourced from the site.

## How to pin

- Pin title = the guide's `seo_title` (already written for search intent)
- Pin description = the guide's dek, then 3–5 plain keywords, no hashtag spam
- Destination = the guide URL, **never** an affiliate link directly. Pinterest
  restricts raw affiliate links; sending traffic to your guide is allowed, keeps
  the click, and the guide is where the affiliate links live anyway.
- 2–5 pins a day beats 30 in one burst.
- Re-pin the same guide later with a different image and title — that is normal
  on Pinterest, not duplication.
